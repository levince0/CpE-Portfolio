My Projects

IoT Management: A Framework for Responsible Artificial Intelligence Water Consumption

This project was given during the first semester of my first year college. In a group of 5, we had to pick an SDG and propose a solution for the chosen SDG using our creativity as Computer Engineers.
Our group chose SDG 9: Innovation, Industry, and Infrastructure for our project. We created a concept that focuses on addressing the inefficient use of water resources caused by Artificial Intelligence (AI) workloads by gathering data through an Internet of Things (IoT) system.

Ticketing System

This project was given during the work immersion of my 12th grade. As a team of ICT students, we were tasked in creating a ticketing system using database and C# for booking tickets and seats at our school's musical play.
The system used MySql for its database and C# as its programming language.
The way it works is that people can choose the seats they want as long as its available.
The UI shows a 1:1 position of the stage or the screen and the seats as a reference for the users.
Once a person picked a seat, the color of the block in the UI will change, indicating that it can't be selected again and must choose a different seat or a different schedule. Additionally, this program was hosted using a local host in the school that was set up by our instructor or supervisor.

My First Three Months in College

In the past 3 months I've been a frosh here in Mapua, I can say that it was fun yet difficult. The tasks, the quizzes, all of it was not easy. Although its been difficult adapting to this new environment, thanks to my classmates, friends, and family, I was able to pick myself up and slowly adjust to these changes.

All these factors is why I believe this program has shaped me technically and personally during my time here in Mapua thus far.
